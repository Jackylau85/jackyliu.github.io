From Product to Real Application

A product specification is only the beginning. In vehicle electronics, the final result depends on how the monitor, camera, SVM box, wiring, connectors, power supply and vehicle environment work together.

My focus is on understanding these connections and identifying practical problems before they become expensive problems during mass production.

Practical Experience

My background combines international logistics and freight export, wiring and cable experience, and several years working with vehicle electronics.

This combination helps me look at vehicle projects not only from a product perspective, but also from manufacturing, sourcing, logistics and customer application perspectives.

About Jacky Liu

I work in international B2B sales and business development, with experience across vehicle electronics, wiring and international trade.

I have around 8 years of experience in international logistics and freight export, around 8 years of experience in wiring and cable-related business, and about 3 years of experience in vehicle electronics.

My vehicle experience is the newest part of my background, so I prefer to focus on practical problems, real applications and continuous learning rather than presenting myself as a purely technical engineer.

Today, my main interests are vehicle monitors, SVM / AVM systems, vehicle safety, camera systems, wiring and connectivity.
