Technical Problems I Help Solve

Some of the questions that often appear when a vehicle vision or safety system moves from prototype to real-world installation.

The image works on the bench — why does it fail in the vehicle?

Installation environment, power, grounding, EMI, cable routing and signal compatibility can change the result.

Which display is suitable for an SVM / AVM system?

The answer depends on camera resolution, interface, brightness, switching, touch requirements and installation conditions.

Why does a camera system become unstable with longer cables?

Cable quality, shielding, connectors, power and signal integrity all become more important as installation distance increases.

What should be considered before selecting a vehicle display?

A display should be considered as part of the complete vehicle system rather than as an isolated screen.
